# Better Hitbox Trail

Better Hitbox Trail adds an in-game settings popup for a more customizable hitbox trail.

## Usage

Open the in-game settings with the configured keybind (default **F7**) for quick tweaks. Per-hitbox and per-game-mode opacity, color, and thickness are configured separately in the mod's Geode settings page.

## Features

- Hitbox Trail
- Show on Death
- Always Show
- Only Player
- Only Cube
- Only Click and Release
- Square Hitbox
- Blue Hitbox
- Circle Hitbox
- Rotation Hitbox
- Trail Length
- Opacity
- Thickness
- Color Clicks
- ...and more

All popup settings take effect immediately, even while paused.

## Notes

Please turn off any built-in Hitbox Trail feature in other mods (QOLMod, MegaHack, Eclipse) to prevent overlap.

If you also use MegaHack, turn off **Player** under MegaHack's Show Hitboxes sub-settings.

Eclipse and QOLMod both show the player hitbox whenever their Show Hitboxes setting is enabled, and there's no way to turn off just that part (Eclipse has a "Hide Player" setting, but it doesn't seem to actually work). Because of this, their player hitboxes will overlap with Better Hitbox Trail.

## Known Issues

- Even if MegaHack's Show Hitboxes is enabled, Better Hitbox Trail may automatically disable itself if MegaHack has no hitboxes of its own to display on screen.
